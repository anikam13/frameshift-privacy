FrameShift Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

FrameShift (“the App”) is an iOS camera app operated by Anirudh Kishore Kamath (“we,” “us,” or “developer”). FrameShift is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means and how it relates to Apple’s App Privacy requirements.

Summary

FrameShift does not collect personal data. Camera frames, reference photos, captured shots, and settings stay on your device. We do not operate a backend server, do not create accounts, and do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. In App Store Connect, FrameShift’s App Privacy answers are declared as Data Not Collected.

Information We Collect

FrameShift does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share any personal information, device identifiers, location data, usage analytics, diagnostics, or crash reports with us or with any third party controlled by us. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no network requests of any kind for product functionality — the App has no backend server to send data to.

We do not use your photos or camera frames for advertising, marketing, model training, or any purpose other than the on-device features described below.

Camera Access

FrameShift requests camera access only to show the live viewfinder and to capture photos when you tap the shutter. Camera frames are analyzed on-device (using Apple’s Vision framework) to draw framing guides. This analysis never leaves your device, and frames are not stored unless you deliberately capture a photo.

Camera permission is requested in context — when you use the camera features — not for unrelated purposes.

Photo Library Access

FrameShift requests two separate, limited permissions:

  • Importing reference photos: handled through Apple’s system photo picker, which runs outside of FrameShift’s process. FrameShift only ever receives the specific photos you choose to import — it never receives broad access to your photo library.
  • Saving your photos: FrameShift can save captured shots and export cards to your camera roll. This is “add-only” access — FrameShift cannot read, browse, or modify the rest of your photo library.

Photos you save to the Photos app remain under your control in iOS. FrameShift does not upload them.

On-Device Processing

When you import a reference photo, FrameShift uses Apple’s Vision framework to analyze it (detecting the subject, pose, and horizon) entirely on your device, in order to build the framing guide shown on the camera screen. No image, video, or analysis result is ever uploaded anywhere by FrameShift.

Data Storage and Retention

Reference photos, captured shots, and your app settings are stored locally in FrameShift’s private storage on your device. Nothing is synced to iCloud or any external server by FrameShift itself. Data remains on your device until you delete it in-app or uninstall the App.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual reference photos or captured shots at any time from within the App, or use “Clear All References” / “Clear All Captured Shots” in Settings to remove everything at once. Deleting the App removes all of its data from your device.

Because we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, there is no server-side copy for us to delete. If you have a privacy question, contact us at the email below.

Third-Party Sharing and Tracking

FrameShift does not share data with any third party. FrameShift contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Note: Apple may process limited information related to App Store distribution, TestFlight testing, or system services under Apple’s own policies. That processing is controlled by Apple, not by FrameShift.

Children’s Privacy

FrameShift does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction), because it does not collect personal information from anyone.
